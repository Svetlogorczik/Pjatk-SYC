Labs

  • 1 Lab 1Introductory class: the lab equipmentThe first class carries no points. You get to know the power supply, the multimeter, the generator and the oscilloscope — four devices you will spend the next five weeks with.
  • 2 Lab 2Measuring voltages and currentsThe first graded class (5 pts). You measure voltage, resistance and current — with a digital meter, an oscilloscope and a needle ammeter — and work out the error of every result.
  • 3 Lab 3Passive components: the impedance of a resistor, a capacitor and an inductorOn the oscilloscope you watch the voltage and the current of a component at the same time. From two sine waves you read two numbers — the amplitude ratio and the shift — and combine them into the impedance as a complex number.
  • 4 Lab 4Passive RC filters: low-pass and high-passYou build two filters from one resistor and one capacitor, measure how much signal gets through at eight frequencies, convert to decibels and read the cut-off frequency from the graph.
  • 5 Lab 5Diodes and transistors: the LED, testing a transistor, the transistor switchYou check which way a diode conducts, use the multimeter to identify a transistor's type, and build two transistor switches — one turned on by a high level, the other by a low level.
  • 6 Lab 6The operational amplifier: follower, comparator, non-inverting and inverting amplifierOn one board, by moving five jumpers, you build four circuits. For the amplifiers you first calculate the gain from the resistors and then check on the oscilloscope whether reality agrees.
  • 7 Lab 7Introduction to digital circuits: Logisim, truth table, gatesFrom this class on there are no more cables or oscilloscopes — you draw circuits in Logisim and see at once whether they work. You learn the path: verbal description → truth table → formula → gate schematic, and finally you build everything from NAND gates only.
  • 8 Lab 8Minimising Boolean functions: the Karnaugh map, NAND and NOR implementationsThe same circuit can be built from fifteen gates or from four. In this class you learn to do the latter: truth table → Karnaugh map → shortest formula → a schematic made of NAND gates only (or NOR gates only).
